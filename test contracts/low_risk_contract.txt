LOW RISK CONTRACT – PROFESSIONAL CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: 1 March 2026

Between Insight Advisory Group GmbH (“Consultant”) and EcoGrid Energy
Ltd. (“Client”).

1.  Services Consultant shall provide technology consulting services.

2.  Term One-year term. Renewal requires mutual written agreement.

3.  Fees Invoices payable within 15 days.

4.  Confidentiality Confidential information shall be protected for five
    years.

5.  Intellectual Property Consultant retains background IP. Client owns
    paid project deliverables.

6.  Indemnification Mutual indemnification for negligence and IP
    infringement.

7.  Limitation of Liability Liability capped at fees paid under the
    applicable Statement of Work.

8.  Termination Either party may terminate for material breach after a
    30-day cure period.

9.  Governing Law Germany.
