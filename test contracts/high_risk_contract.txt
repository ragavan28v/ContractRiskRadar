HIGH RISK CONTRACT – EQUIPMENT LEASING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: 1 March 2026

Between Metro Construction Rentals Ltd. (“Lessor”) and SkyBuild
Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. (“Lessee”).

1.  Equipment Lessor shall lease heavy construction equipment identified
    in Schedule A.

2.  Term Five-year term with automatic two-year renewals unless Lessee
    gives 120 days’ notice.

3.  Rent Monthly rent: USD 85,000. Payment due within 15 days.

4.  Maintenance Lessee bears all maintenance, insurance, licensing,
    transportation, storage, and repair costs.

5.  Damage Lessee is responsible for all loss or damage until equipment
    is returned.

6.  Insurance Lessee shall maintain all-risk insurance naming Lessor as
    additional insured.

7.  Default Failure to pay within five days constitutes default.

8.  Remedies Lessor may repossess equipment and accelerate all remaining
    payments.

9.  Indemnification Lessee shall indemnify Lessor against all claims,
    penalties, fines, injuries, environmental liabilities, recalls, and
    legal fees.

10. Limitation of Liability Lessor shall not be liable for equipment
    failure, delays, or consequential damages.

11. Governing Law Texas, USA.
