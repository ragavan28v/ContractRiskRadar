MODERATE RISK CONTRACT – SOFTWARE MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

Effective Date: 1 March 2026

Between NextGen Systems Ltd. (“Provider”) and Harbor Logistics PLC
(“Customer”).

1.  Services Provider shall provide software maintenance and support.

2.  Term Two-year initial term with automatic annual renewal unless 60
    days’ notice is given.

3.  Fees Quarterly invoices payable within 30 days.

4.  Service Levels Target uptime of 99.5%.

5.  Confidentiality Confidentiality obligations survive for three years.

6.  Intellectual Property Provider retains software ownership. Customer
    receives a license.

7.  Liability Aggregate liability limited to fees paid during the
    previous 12 months.

8.  Termination Either party may terminate after an uncured material
    breach.

9.  Governing Law England and Wales.
